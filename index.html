HOW THE MODEL WORKS

Simple rules. Visible consequences.

01 / FORAGE Creatures move toward food they can see. Moving and sensing spend energy.

02 / REPRODUCE Well-fed adults can produce offspring. A child inherits speed and sight; either trait may mutate.

03 / SELECT Creatures die when they run out of energy or reach old age. Food availability changes which traits help.

This is an educational model of selection, not a biological prediction. Population and resource limits keep the simulation responsive.
